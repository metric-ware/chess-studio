Pieces are chess.com's neo PNGs from images.chesscomfiles.com/chess-themes/pieces/<set>/<size>/<w|b><p|n|b|r|q|k>.png; 300px is the largest size served and a browser User-Agent is required. Move sounds are chess.com's default MP3 theme (assets/sfx/cc_*.mp3); other effects are synthesized in audio.sfx (whoosh, whoosh_down, impact, boom, subdrop, riser, ding, heartbeat). The only font is Inter Tight (InterTight[wght].ttf from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/intertight, bundled as InterTight.ttf with its OFL licence). It replaced Montserrat in the latest typography revision on 2026-10-07; Fraunces and Barlow Condensed had been removed earlier. Classification icons were removed earlier when nothing used them; they came from archive.org's chesscom-analysis-icons item. Music is MP3 plus JSON source metadata in assets/music; commercial tracks were accepted by the user for Shorts, and the NCS tracks were fetched for the deleted long-form.
