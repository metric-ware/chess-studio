Text uses Pillow because Cairo cannot use the bundled fonts directly. The sole family is Inter Tight, a variable wght font from Google Fonts under OFL; display is weight 800 and label 550. It replaced Montserrat after the latest 2026-10-07 request for improved fonts. White #f2f2f2 and one accent per video (red #ed3e40 in the king hunt, violet #b07aff in the Légal trap) retain the reference channels' colour hierarchy. The user wants chess.com's brilliant teal only on brilliant moves, never as a general caption accent; the Légal trap's first cut used teal for every caption and was changed. Captions have a subtle 2px dark shadow and no coloured glow. SAN keeps its case (Qxh7+!!, Kd2, a1).
text_layout centres and wraps words, preserving [accent] runs within a word. Widths and glyph positions preserve kerning, including tracked characters and accent boundaries; tight canvas bounds come from actual glyph ink relative to the baseline rather than the font's ascender/descender box. Default line spacing is 1.12 times font size and maximum line width 940px. Partial word sprites share the full layout's canvas so stagger animation cannot reflow the caption.
Default reveal animates the whole line with a 12px rise over up to 0.18s. Pop moves from 0.96 to 1 scale without overshoot over up to 0.16s. Both shorten their entrance for brief captions. Optional stagger uses a 12px rise over 0.18s with 0.035s word spacing; none is static. Exit fade is eased over up to 0.1s, or out="cut". The king hunt's hook is visible on frame 0 and uses 62px type in two lines; the Légal trap's uses 72px with the first line on frame 0 and the second popping on the hook's knight landing, and its board opening shows a 29px notation line that grows move by move with its left edge fixed, plus shadow 3 because its close board shots sit under the captions. Supporting labels are 29px with tracking 0.025. Captions render after layers and vignette; Edit(shade=px) supplies a top gradient over the picture when needed.
